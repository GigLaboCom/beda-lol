## What this is

beda.lol is the site of the «Яхта Беда» (Yacht “Beda”) rubric: a quiz, games and a tracker. A registry of projects and a wall of epitaphs for abandoned projects come later. By using the site you accept these rules. How we handle data is described in the [privacy policy](/en/konfidencialnost).

## Your account

- You sign in with GitHub, Google, X or by email. Signing in different ways with the same email address leads to one account.
- You are responsible for access to your email and to the accounts you sign in with.
- You can delete your account in the [cabinet](/en/kabinet), in one click.

## What you may post

When the site gets posts (projects, epitaphs), simple rules apply to them:

- your own projects only;
- no one else's personal data;
- no advertising of services;
- no insults, harassment or threats;
- no spam and no links to malicious content.

Posts are text only. Links in posts are marked for search engines as user-generated.

## Moderation

Posts are reviewed. We may reject or hide a post, and we will tell you why. Repeated breaches get the account blocked.

## Your texts

You keep the rights to what you write. By publishing a post you give the site a free, non-exclusive right to show it on beda.lol and in social media cards until it is deleted. Once you delete a post, it is no longer shown. You delete your own post yourself, in one click.

## Complaints

If a post breaks the rules or concerns you, write to [complaints address]. We answer within 48 hours.

## The site as it is

The quiz and the games are a joke and a nudge to think about your project, not advice. The site is provided as is. We try to keep everything working, but we do not promise it will run without interruptions, and we may change or remove features.

## Brand

The yacht, the style and the texts of the site were made for the rubric. No characters, quotes or songs from cartoons are used here.
